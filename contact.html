Reporting Bugs

Found something broken? We appreciate detailed bug reports. When reporting an issue, it helps if you include:

  • What happened — describe the unexpected behavior.
  • What you expected — what should have happened instead.
  • Device & browser — e.g., "iPhone 15, Safari" or "Windows 11, Chrome."
  • Steps to reproduce — how can we trigger the same issue?
  • Screenshots — if possible, include a screenshot of the problem.

Feature Requests

Have an idea that would make Scoundrel better? We're always interested in hearing suggestions. Some features we've been asked about include:

  • Multiplayer or competitive leaderboards
  • New difficulty modes or game variants
  • Customizable card designs or themes
  • Achievement or unlock systems
  • Tutorial or guided walkthrough for new players

While we can't guarantee every suggestion will be implemented, all feedback is read and considered for future updates.

Quick Note This is a fan-made adaptation of the original Scoundrel card game by Zach Gage and Kurt Bieg. For inquiries about the original game or its official versions, please contact the original creators directly.
